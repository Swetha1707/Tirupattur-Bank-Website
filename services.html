Safe Deposit Lockers

NEFT, RTGS & IMPS

Fast and secure money transfers to bank accounts across India.
  • NEFT available 24×7
  • RTGS for ₹2 lakh and above
  • IMPS for instant transfers
  • Secure bank-to-bank payments

Safe Deposit Lockers

Safe Deposit Locker facility is available for customers at the headquarters and Tamil Nadu Housing Board branches.
  • Small, medium and large lockers
  • Secure strong room facility
  • Annual locker rental
  • Subject to availability and applicable RBI rules

RuPay Debit Card

Convenient access to your account for ATM withdrawals and everyday payments.
  • Cash withdrawal at ATMs
  • Shop and online payments
  • Secure PIN-based transactions
  • Wide RuPay acceptance

SMS Alerts

Stay informed about important transactions through instant SMS notifications.
  • Deposit alerts
  • Withdrawal alerts
  • Transfer alerts
  • Helps monitor account activity

Demand Drafts & Pay Orders

Secure payment instruments for official, educational and business-related payments.
  • Demand draft issuance
  • Pay order issuance
  • Useful for fees and tenders
  • Same-day service subject to requirements

UPI & Mobile Banking

Manage your account and make convenient digital payments through supported services.
  • Instant UPI payments
  • QR code payments
  • Money transfer
  • Account balance and transaction access

Cheque Clearing (CTS)

Efficient cheque processing through the Cheque Truncation System.
  • Electronic cheque processing
  • Faster clearing
  • Secure transaction handling
  • Cheque status tracking

Insurance & Government Schemes

Access eligible insurance and government-backed financial schemes through the bank.
  • PMJJBY enrolment
  • PMSBY enrolment
  • APY enrolment
  • Scheme-based eligibility

Pension & DBT Credit

Receive eligible pensions and government benefits directly into your bank account.
  • Pension credit
  • Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
  • Government subsidy credits
  • Direct account-based payments
