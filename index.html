Why bank with a co-operative

Owned by members

Every shareholder is a customer from our area. Profits return to members as dividend and to the town as lower loan rates.

Decisions made here

Loans are sanctioned by a local board that understands the mango season, the market days and the needs of small traders.

Safe and regulated

We are supervised by the Reserve Bank of India, and every depositor is insured by DICGC up to ₹5 lakh.
