Our journey

A century of banking, built by the town

The Kaveripattinam Cooperative Town Bank Ltd. No. 5918, from registration to today.

  1. 16 March 1921

    Bank registered

    The Kaveripattinam Cooperative Town Bank Ltd. No. 5918 was registered.

  2. 22 June 1921

    Operations begin

    The bank commenced its operations and opened its doors to members.

  3. Today

    Two branches

    Functioning from Kaveripattinam and Vadamalampatti (Pochampalli).

  4. 31 Mar 2026

    12,889 members

    'A' Class membership stands at 12,889, holding a share capital of ₹250.86 lakhs.

  5. 27 Sep 2026

    Banking licence

    The Reserve Bank of India issued the banking licence.

  6. Coming next

    New branches

    A proposal to open a new branch at Uthangarai and Mathur is under process.
