Gold Loan

நகைக் கடன்

11% p.a.

Get up to ₹10,000 per gram against your gold ornaments, appraised at the counter in front of you. The most requested loan at our branch.

  • Up to ₹10,000 per gram of gold
  • Loan up to RBI-permitted loan-to-value
  • You can pay the principal and interest at any time
  • No prepayment charges
  • Gold stored in insured vault
  • Renewal without re-pledging
You will need: Aadhaar card, PAN card, one photograph and the gold ornaments.

House Construction Loan

வீட்டுக் கட்டுமானக் கடன்

13% p.a.

Build, extend or repair your home, with funds released in stages as construction progresses.

  • Construction, extension or repair
  • Stage-wise disbursement
  • Repayment up to 15 years
  • No hidden charges
  • Interest calculated on the reducing balance as you repay EMIs
You will need: KYC, income proof, sale deed or patta or Land Register or Original Deed, approved building plan, estimate and encumbrance certificate.

Home Mortgage Loan

வீடு அடமானக் கடன்

13% p.a.

Raise funds against your property for business, education or personal needs, secured by a registered mortgage.

  • Loan against property title
  • Flexible repayment tenure
  • Attractive interest rate
  • Part-prepayment allowed
  • Interest calculated on the reducing balance as you repay EMIs
You will need: Aadhaar card, PAN card, property title deed,sale deed or patta or Land Register or Original Deed, encumbrance certificate and income proof.

Loan for Differently Abled Persons (NHFDC)

மாற்றுத்திறனாளிகளுக்கான கடன் (NHFDC)

15% p.a.

A concessional loan channelled through NHFDC to help persons with disabilities start or expand a livelihood activity.

  • Concessional interest rate
  • Flexible repayment tenure
  • Collateral/guarantor as applicable
  • Priority processing
You will need: Aadhaar card, PAN card, disability certificate and a project or business proposal.

Self Help Group Loan

சுய உதவிக் குழுக் கடன்

15% p.a.

Credit for registered Self Help Groups to fund income-generating activities for their members.

  • Group-based lending
  • Flexible repayment tenure
  • No collateral for eligible groups
  • Linked group savings account required
You will need: SHG registration/bye-laws, resolution copy and KYC of authorised signatories.

TABCEDCO Loan

TABCEDCO கடன்

7% p.a.

A subsidised loan routed through TABCEDCO for Eligible Backward Classes and Most Backward Classes beneficiaries to start or grow a livelihood activity.

  • Subsidised interest rate
  • Government-linked scheme
  • Flexible repayment tenure
  • Priority processing
You will need: Aadhaar card, PAN card, community certificate and a project or business proposal.

TAMCO Loan

TAMCO கடன்

6% p.a.

A subsidised loan routed through TAMCO for eligible minority community beneficiaries to start or grow a livelihood activity.

  • Subsidised interest rate
  • Government-linked scheme
  • Flexible repayment tenure
  • Priority processing
You will need: Aadhaar card, PAN card, community certificate and a project or business proposal.

Women Entrepreneur Loan

பெண் தொழில்முனைவோர் கடன்

15% p.a.

Credit for women starting or expanding a business, trade or service venture.

  • Flexible repayment tenure
  • Attractive interest rate
  • Collateral as applicable
  • Priority service at the counter
You will need: Aadhaar card, PAN card, business proof or proposal and address proof.

Working Women Loan

பணிபுரியும் பெண்களுக்கான கடன்

14% p.a.

Personal credit for salaried women employees to meet planned expenses, repaid through monthly salary deduction.

  • Salary-linked eligibility
  • Flexible repayment tenure
  • Minimal paperwork
  • Attractive interest rate
You will need: Aadhaar card, PAN card, salary certificate and latest payslips.

Surety Loan

பிணையக் கடன்

15% p.a.

A personal loan secured by the guarantee of one or more sureties instead of collateral, For salaried individuals and small and micro-business owners.

  • No collateral required
  • Guarantor-backed
  • Flexible repayment tenure
  • Quick processing
You will need: Aadhaar card, PAN card, income proof and KYC of the surety/guarantor.
