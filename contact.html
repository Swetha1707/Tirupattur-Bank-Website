Getting here

Kaveripattinam is on National Highway 44, about 10 km south of Krishnagiri and 30 km from Dharmapuri. Town buses from Krishnagiri stop on Salem Main Road.

From Krishnagiri

About 15 minutes by road along NH 44 towards Salem.
